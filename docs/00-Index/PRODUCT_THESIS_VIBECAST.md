# Product thesis — VibeCast (GCS wing)

## One-liner

**Play or talk once → daily social + podcast draft + publish hold — without editing on the couch.**

Internal home: **GCS** (Gaming Content Studio) · wing **VibeCast** · brand **TWE** (and later twins).  
Not a second studio. Not auto-post spam.

---

## Who cannot live without this (when it works)

| Persona | Job to be done | Failure without us |
|---------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Streamer / vibe-caster** | Turn real play energy into posts without a producer | Hard drive full of VODs, zero dailies |
| **Podcaster / essay voice** | Capture house-friendly talk and leave with chapters + show notes | Endless “I’ll edit later” |
| **Social multi-poster** | One package, many platforms, **human go** | Scheduler anxiety or per-account cost spiral |
| **Returner / niche brand** | Same voice across factory art **and** personal play | Two brands that never meet |
| **No-cam / quiet chat streamer** | Mascot companion (e.g. **Rotgut**) that answers chat | Dead air + “just another POV” |

---

## Pains → must-haves

| Pain | Must-have | We have? |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Hours recorded, nothing postable | Vibe → package pipeline | Scaffold · needs dogfood night |
| Blank mic anxiety | Mode cards + soft prompts | Yes |
| Accidental auto-post | Fail-closed Armory | Yes |
| 12 apps, no spine | One citadel + glossary | Yes (docs) |
| Broken audio discovered at edit | 60s gate + stamp | Playbook yes · stamp open |
| Clips / stills / captions separate jobs | Inter-promo one night | Map yes · attach script next |
| Can’t hand off to an assistant | Agent walk protocol | Yes |
| Posting fleet too expensive | Local outbox + optional vendor | MVP yes |
| Factory content soulless alone | Factory + Vibe under one brand | Doctrine yes |

---

## Moat (why this is sticky)

1. **Play-first** — product starts at the keyboard, not CapCut  
2. **Fail-closed publish** — trust for real accounts  
3. **Agent-operable** — walk-throughs + harvest, not lonely SaaS  
4. **Factory + Vibe** — volume art + personality under one brand  
5. **Local Armory** — cost independence when multipost vendors hurt  

---

## What we are *not*

- Another pure editor  
- Another pure scheduler  
- Unbounded AI clip spam from empty folders  
- Auto-live Twitch requirement  
- A second brain vault with no package exit
