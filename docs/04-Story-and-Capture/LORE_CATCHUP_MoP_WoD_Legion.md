## MoP — Mists of Pandaria

**Optional split file:** [[catch-up-rails/CATCHUP_RAIL_MOP|CATCHUP_RAIL_MOP]]

### Cold-open line

> “You log in after the Lich King and the next big pitch is… bamboo, jade, and a monk class. Your Wrath brain goes ‘is this a side expansion?’ — and then the story quietly becomes one of the most serious faction tragedies the game ever told.”

### Friend-explains bullets (Wrath-brain lens)

1. **Tone shock (public high-level).** After Icecrown’s gothic finality, MoP *looks* soft — new continent, Pandaren, martial-arts fantasy. The “cute” first impression is real and fair.
2. **What the plot actually does (public high-level, no quest dump).** Horde and Alliance crash into Pandaria chasing war; old powers wake; **faction leadership cracks**. Sha ≈ “negative emotion made monster.” Garrosh’s arc turns travelogue into civil war.
3. **Wrath contrast.** Wrath: clear monster-king, you are coalition hero. MoP: the continent is not the only villain — **we** import the war. Name that out loud for Mac.
4. **Systems that stuck (public high-level).** Monk class · scenarios / short story chunks · farm/daily-hub “life sim” energy · challenge-mode timer culture (ancestor vibe for modern timed dungeons) · Timeless Isle–style open sandbox end energy (optional name).
5. **What a cold Wrath vet misses first.** Not every rare. The **emotional turn**: discovery → “why are we still fighting here?” → Orgrimmar-as-raid climax energy. If you only remember “pandas,” you missed the second half.
6. **Character bridge (vault).** No dedicated MoP campaign log. Optional class-flavor only: Agony **green fire** interest (spooky taxonomy — not expansion story SoT). MoP campaign = **shared blank** — honest track gold.
7. **Duo angle.** Beautiful open zones, story you can react to together without mythic context. Chromie MoP later = optional play, not required for audio.
8. **Takeaway.** MoP is where modern WoW learned a “funny continent” can still deliver **political tragedy** — and systems experiments outlive the patch cycle.

### Mac / Macraven ask-me prompts

1. First reaction if I only said “new expansion: land of pandaren” — joke or intrigue?  
2. Garrosh in your head: *Warchief you trusted* or *already the problem*?  
3. Prefer **war story** or **farm / chill systems** first?  
4. Chromie MoP later: monk alt or tourist zones?  
5. What Wrath memory would feel *weird* next to jade forests and temples?

### Bridge to Midnight / 2026

Pandaria is the first big post-Wrath lesson: **the map can feel alien and the war can still be about us.** Later expansions keep “new texture, same faction gravity” until the story pivots toward world-soul / void-adjacent talk people name when they say **Midnight**. We don’t need MoP completionism for 2026 — we need permission that **missing a whole continent is normal**, and that our characters can carry later scars (especially Legion) without farming every Pandaria daily.

## WoD — Warlords of Draenor

**Optional split file:** [[catch-up-rails/CATCHUP_RAIL_WOD|CATCHUP_RAIL_WOD]]

### Cold-open line

> “Warlords says: what if we time-travel to Draenor *before* it became Outland, fight the orc clans at full power, and live in a fortress that builds itself? For a lot of people it was the most exciting trailer and the expansion where the sub quietly expired.”

### Friend-explains bullets (Wrath-brain lens)

1. **Tone (public high-level).** Muscular, cinematic, “war movie.” Alternate-timeline Draenor; Iron Horde tech-and-clan fantasy. Feels closer to classic orc myth than Pandaria’s discovery vibe.
2. **Story spine (public high-level).** Post-MoP Garrosh helps rewrite history; you fight warlords who are legends, not yet the fel-corrupted Outland you remember from BC. Familiar names, wrong era — that dissonance *is* the expansion.
3. **Wrath brain friction.** Wrath: push Northrend, kill Arthas. WoD: big war trailer → day-to-day often **garrison management** + zone hubs. Name the trailer-vs-Tuesday gap without a rant.
4. **Systems that stuck (public high-level).** Garrisons / player-housing-as-hub DNA · follower / mission-table culture · late naval-mission flavor (name only) · raid spectacle names (Highmaul / Blackrock Foundry / Hellfire Citadel — names, not guides).
5. **“Why people left” — no snark walls (public consensus talking points).** Content droughts · garrison success → isolation · flying/exploration friction · story promise vs middle sag · social bleed (“empty garrison” loneliness meme). Tone: “what the room felt like,” not dunking on players.
6. **Modern chat stamp.** “I quit in WoD” is still a **calendar stamp**. Also: some of the era’s best raid *spectacle*. Both can be true in one sentence.
7. **Character bridge (vault).** **No dedicated WoD campaign evidence.** Honest: we may have barely touched this. Contrast Legion (Deathlord, DH class) — WoD is the gap we admit without shame.
8. **Takeaway.** Missing content is not a moral failure — sometimes the structure made it easy to walk away, and the story continued without you.

4. Is modern housing fixing a want garrisons almost answered?  
5. What would make a “dry patch” kill your sub *today* vs then?

## Legion

**Optional split file:** [[catch-up-rails/CATCHUP_RAIL_LEGION|CATCHUP_RAIL_LEGION]]  
**Public OK?** Confirm before naming **Deathlord / Apocalypse** on a public track (Kyle fill-in below).

### Cold-open line

> “Legion is the expansion where the Burning Legion stops being a Burning Crusade memory and becomes a full-scale invasion of Azeroth — and for our roster, it isn’t abstract: Deathlord, Apocalypse, Demon Hunter, Order Hall energy are still stamped on the characters we log.”

### Friend-explains bullets (Wrath-brain lens)

1. **Tone (public high-level).** Heroic, class-fantasy maximalist, “we might actually lose.” Broken Isles as multi-zone warfront; Dalaran floating hub again (Wrath Dalaran *moved*). Illidan reframed beyond “BC villain.”
2. **Story spine for a Wrath brain (public high-level).** Legion is *here*, not “out there on Outland.” Ancient scars reopen. Late-exp Argus = take the war to their homeworld (scale jump people still cite). Feelings-first; no raid-wing checklist.
3. **Artifacts (public high-level + vault).** Each spec got a **named legendary weapon identity**. **Vault:** Crimsonpain’s **Apocalypse** + **Deathlord** = walked the DK Order Hall / campaign deep enough that the account still wears it. Series gold: *we don’t only talk Legion; one of us finished a chapter.*
4. **Order Halls / class campaign (public high-level).** Class home + “you are the high-water mark of this order.” Deathlord (DK) · Netherlord fantasy lane (Warlock / Agony) · DH starting experience.
5. **Systems that stuck (public high-level).** Mythic+ as durable dungeon sport · world quests language · class mounts / identity rewards · transmog long-tail. **Vault:** Havoc still holds Legion-era mats residue (routing map) — inventory “remembers.”
6. **Demon Hunters (public high-level + vault).** Hero class: fel dual-wield, metamorphosis, starter that *is* the fantasy. **Vault:** **Crimsonhavoc** is a live DH on Thrall — Legion’s class invention sits on the duo board.
7. **Wrath contrast.** Wrath: one DK starting story, then shared war. Legion: **every class** gets a prestige arc; war shared, identity personalized. Why many still call it a favorite.
8. **Takeaway.** Check title / weapon / DH alt before calling Legion a total blank. Also design ancestor of “modern chores” (WQ, M+) people love or negotiate in 2026.
