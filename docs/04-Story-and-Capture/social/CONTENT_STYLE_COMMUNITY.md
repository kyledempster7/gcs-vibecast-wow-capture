## Who we show up for

| Welcome | Energy |
|---------|--------|
| **New players** | Overwhelmed is normal; orientation without shame |
| **Returning players** | Wrath-brain / “the room rearranged” honesty |
| **Casual / gather / story** | Herb nights, small wins, level-from-gather joy |
| **Community** | Explorer’s League welcome · Thrall someday (never invent roster) |

**PvP players are welcome as people.** Content **strategy** is not PVP-elitist or min-max shaming.

---

## Tone

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Fun · upbeat · positive | Ragebait · channel-vs-Blizzard |
| Friend leveling next to you | “Casual” as insult · noob-shaming |
| Playful herb-rival laughs | Doxx · harass · mock other players by name |
| Small wins (3 levels from herbs) | Flex stacks / gold brags as identity |
| Soft invite: “got this too?” | “Git gud or leave” · toxic min-max |

---

## Caption templates (signals only — fill from disk)

Use only when the night actually had that beat. Never invent the rival race/class or level count.

| Beat | Seed (edit with real facts) |
|------|-----------------------------|
| Gather night | “Returner herb night. Just gathering. Still leveling. Still fun.” |
| Level-from-gather | “Leveled from herbing today. Modern WoW can be weird like that — in a good way.” |
| Shared node (funny) | “Someone else wanted the same herb. Shared forest energy. We both get to laugh.” |
| Overwhelmed returner | “If the client feels huge after years away — same. One system at a time.” |
| Welcome | “New or back? You’re not late. You’re here.” |

Soft footer optional: `Returner energy · Thrall · Horde` — never invent League members.

---

## Banned in drafts

- Invented achievements, levels, zones, rivals, dialogue  
- Toxic ranking of player skill as content thesis  
- Silent publish / arm without kyle_go  
- Guest call-ins / rage bait  
- Class-P letter automation mixed into daily social without explicit product switch
