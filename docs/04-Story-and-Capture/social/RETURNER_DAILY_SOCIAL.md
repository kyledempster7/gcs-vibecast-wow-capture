# Returner Daily — social product (not Class-P)

**Name:** **Returner Daily**  
**Do not call this Class-P.** Class-P is a separate TWE letter / editorial lane. This product is the **daily social surface** for play + capture.

**Kyle job:** play · optional talk (clean or muddy) · full logout.  
**Agent job:** harvest · clip · assemble · draft.  
**Publish:** human go only · no auto-Zernio freestyle.

## The three daily shapes

| # | Shape | What it is | Fuel |
|---|--------|------------|------|
| **1 · Carousel** | Multi-slide post (IG / similar) | **Main daily social unit** | Talk clips + stills + Memento |
| **2 · B-roll** | Short visual cuts | Atmosphere / systems / travel | OBS / Process B-Roll / FOOTAGE map |
| **3 · Personality** | Snippets of **you** | Voice + face-of-play energy | Muddy or clean AV of you playing/talking |

These three can **feed each other** (a personality clip becomes carousel slide 1; a Memento still becomes slide 2). They are not three separate full-time jobs for Kyle.

## Carousel recipe (default — “waiting room” scale)

**Intent:** feel like a small daily chapter — not a 12-minute EP, not a live stream to a crowd.

| Slide | Type | Source | Role |
|------:|------|--------|------|
| 1 | **Video** | Clip from play+talk (when you spoke · character on screen) | Personality + moment |
| 2 | **Image** | Screenshot / **Memento** achievement or popup still | “What just happened” |
| 3+ | Optional | More clip · another Memento · soft caption card · achievement-frame later | Only if real fuel |

**Minimum viable carousel:** **1 video + 1 still** (if either side is empty, ship **story single** or skip day — blanks beat fakes).

**Tone:** nostalgia · learning the modern game · friend on the side (Mac silent) · someday guild/community on **Thrall** — never forced hype.

**Not required:** live streaming to an audience. Private play+talk with one friend is valid fuel.

## Memento (character achievements → photos)

**What it is:** addon that photographs real in-game moments (achievements, interval, level-up delay, etc.).  
**Why it matters:** ties social stills to **your characters actually achieving something** — not freehand AI glory shots.

| Account WTF | `…\_retail_\WTF\Account\CERBERUS321` present |

**Daily use:** after play night, agents list **new** Screenshots by mtime → candidate stills for carousel slide 2+.
