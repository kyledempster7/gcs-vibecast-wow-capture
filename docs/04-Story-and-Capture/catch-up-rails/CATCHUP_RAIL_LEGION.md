## Talking bullets (5–8)

1. **Tone (public high-level).** Heroic, class-fantasy maximalist, “we might actually lose.” Broken Isles as a warfront of zones; Dalaran as floating hub again (nostalgia for Wrath Dalaran *moved*). Illidan stops being only BC villain and becomes a complicated spearpoint.

2. **Story spine for a Wrath brain (public high-level).**  
   - The Legion is *here*, not “out there on Outland.”  
   - Pillars / tombs / ancient night elf and titan scars get reopened.  
   - Argus late-exp: you take the war to the Legion’s homeworld — scale jump people still cite.  
   - Keep it feelings-first; no raid wing checklist.

3. **Artifacts = the class fantasy hook that still matters (public high-level + vault).**  
   - Each spec got a **legendary weapon identity** (not just higher stats — a *named* weapon story).  
   - Modern chat still uses artifact names as shorthand for “who you were in Legion.”  
   - **Vault:** Crimsonpain’s weapon **Apocalypse** + title **Deathlord** = he walked the **DK Order Hall / campaign** deep enough that the account still wears it. That is series gold: *we don’t only talk Legion; one of us finished a chapter of it.*

4. **Order Halls / class campaign (public high-level).** Your class got a home, a follower cast, and a “you are the high-water mark of this order” arc. **Deathlord** (DK), **Netherlord** (Warlock — Agony fantasy lane), Demon Hunter starting experience — these titles and halls still show up in transmog, mounts, and “what did you main in Legion?” conversations.

5. **Systems that stuck (public high-level).**  
   - **Mythic+** as a durable endgame product (born/popularized in this era’s dungeon culture).  
   - **World quests** as the open-world daily replacement language.  
   - **Class mounts / class identity rewards** people still chase years later.  
   - **Transmog / appearance** as a long-tail reason to revisit.  
   - **Vault note:** Havoc holds **Legion-era mats** in bags (routing map) — even inventory still says “this expansion left residue.”

6. **Demon Hunters (public high-level + vault).** Brand-new hero class: dual-wield fel, metamorphosis, starting scenario that *is* the class fantasy. **Vault:** **Crimsonhavoc** is a live Demon Hunter on Thrall — Legion’s class invention is on the duo stakeout board, not just lore.

7. **Wrath contrast.** Wrath: one death knight starting story, then you join the same war as everyone. Legion: **every class** gets a prestige arc; the war is shared but the *identity* is personalized. That is why Legion still dominates “favorite expansion” lists for many.

8. **Takeaway line.** For a Wrath vet, Legion is the gap expansion that might not be a blank — **check the title, the weapon, the DH alt** — and it is also the design ancestor of “modern WoW chores” (WQ, M+) people either love or negotiate with in 2026.

## Bridge to Midnight

Legion is the strongest **personal** bridge on this shelf: our warband already carries its stamps. After Legion, public story rolls through faction war (BfA), the afterlife (Shadowlands — **vault play lane** for Pain + Mac), dragon recovery (Dragonflight), and the world-soul / underground arc into **War Within → Midnight** conversation.
