## Bridge to Midnight (timeline version)

Wrath taught us the *scale* of a world-ending villain. Everything after is variations on “the world breaks again, but the *texture* of the break changes” — empire vs empire, alternate timelines, full Legion invasion, then death’s bureaucracy, then recovery, then the world-soul arc people name when they say **Midnight**. Our job in this series is not to memorize the arc. It is to notice that our **characters already carry pieces** of the gap (especially Legion), and that playing together in 2026 is walking back into a conversation that never stopped just because we did.
