## Series premise

Kyle is a **Wrath of the Lich King (WoTLK) veteran** who largely **missed or stopped during Pandaria (MoP), Warlords of Draenor (WoD), and Legion**. In **2026** he returns with friend **Mac** / **Macraven** (same person) into the **The War Within / Midnight** era.

**“Midnight” framing:** Kyle’s named 2026 return frame. Vault already treats retail as **Midnight era** for housing/décor notes (~12.0.x). Treat **War Within** as the prior modern expansion band; do not invent patch claims on mic—verify against play or vault facts first.

**Core promise:** Multiple **linkable audio tracks** that map an emotional + lore arc—friend-explains-to-friend, duo vibes, first-nights-back systems shock, Midnight hooks for a Wrath brain—so Kyle can record between sessions without restarting a monologue.
