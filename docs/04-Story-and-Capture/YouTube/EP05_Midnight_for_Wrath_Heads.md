## Talking bullets (5–8) — gap years → 2026 return

Record **feelings**, not a second EP02. Aim ~90 sec each; skip any bullet that deadens the room.

1. **Era frame, not patch notes (vault + public high-level).**  
   “Midnight” is Kyle’s **2026 return banner**. Vault housing notes already sit in **Midnight era** retail (~12.0.x language in Housing pack) — use as “we are in *that chapter of the client*,” **not** a feature walkthrough.  
   **War Within → Midnight** = conversation **band** (public high-level). What a Wrath head needs is *why to log in this week*, not a season ID.

2. **MoP bridge harvest (public high-level → feeling).**  
   First post-Wrath lesson: **the map can feel alien and the war can still be about us.** Missing a whole continent is **normal**. You do not need Pandaria completionism to stand in 2026 chat.  
   *Do not* retell Garrosh/sha here — point off-mic to rail if Mac wants depth later.

3. **WoD bridge harvest (public high-level → permission).**  
   WoD’s scar is **permission to leave**. A Midnight return is allowed to be **selective**. We do not owe garrison years a full clear. Name the gap; keep the friendship; play the chapter that has heat.

4. **Legion bridge harvest (vault where labeled).**  
   Strongest **personal** bridge on the shelf: characters already carry stamps.  
   - **Vault:** Crimsonpain — Legion **Deathlord** / **Apocalypse** path (onesheet) — **confirm public OK** before naming on mic.  
   - **Vault:** Crimsonhavoc — live **Demon Hunter** (Legion-born class on roster).  
   - After Legion (public high-level spine only): BfA → SL → DF → world-soul / underground talk into War Within → **Midnight** as horizon word.  
   Pain’s onesheet energy: **mid-journey**, not “Midnight main tonight.” Series can name the horizon while play stays one Chromie chapter open.

5. **Wrath rhyme hooks (mixed).**  
   Things a Wrath brain can grab **without** finishing modern campaigns:  
   - **Death / undead fantasy still has a chair** — **vault:** Pain Unholy DK identity; Forsaken/Lordaeron **housing** fantasy (wishlist exists — *do not* read the full décor list on mic).  
   - **Duo exploration over raid FOMO** — **vault:** live seats, not mythic obligation.  
   - **Story you can feel in a zone** — Howling / SL start / open-world pets energy over meta spreadsheet.

6. **Honest multi-road return (vault).**  
   Mid-band alts + multi-expansion client = you can still choose **old roads** (Howling spirit, SL start pack, Chromie chapter) while the modern client runs Midnight-era systems (warband, Collections, housing — EP04 territory; name only).  
   **Veteran advantage:** you already know how to **care about a place**.

7. **Timeline one-liner (public high-level).**  
   Wrath taught the *scale* of a world-ending villain. Everything after is variations on “the world breaks again, but the *texture* of the break changes.” Our job is not to memorize the arc — it is to notice our characters already carry pieces of the gap, and that playing together in 2026 is walking back into a conversation that never stopped.

8. **Optional soft housing nod (vault — light only).**  
   Housing exists in this era; undead/DK/Lordaeron décor is a **play lane** in the vault, not homework for this track. One sentence max: “There’s a home-base fantasy again — and for a DK brain, that’s delicious.”  
   **TODO probe:** any *new* Midnight housing system features beyond “era + catalog exists” → verify in-game before claiming.
